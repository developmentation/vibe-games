Better, faster and smarter access to government services.

  • Overview
  • Support

Overview

The Alberta Wallet is a secure, private and convenient way to carry your Government of Alberta-issued documents.

This initiative is part of Alberta’s broader efforts to modernize government service delivery and make access to public services more convenient for Albertans.

The Alberta Wallet is available online through your Alberta.ca Account or as a mobile app for Android and iOS. 

The Mobile Health Card is the first document available in the Alberta Wallet. More documents will be added in the future.

Benefits

The Alberta Wallet is designed with Albertans in mind and offers:

Choice

It's optional, not a requirement. You can continue to use your physical cards if you prefer – it’s your choice!

Security

Protection provided by advanced encryption to ensure your personal data stays safe. Only you can access your Wallet, and no information is shared without your permission.

Convenience

Securely access your Government of Alberta-issued documents directly on your mobile device. No more searching for physical cards or waiting for replacements in the mail.

Privacy

Your privacy and personal data are protected. The Wallet is designed to meet Alberta’s strict privacy laws, and national and international privacy and security standards. 

Durability

Unlike paper or plastic cards, Alberta Wallet documents don’t get damaged or wear out. They stay intact on your mobile device and are accessible online via the Alberta.ca Account.

Simplicity

It’s simple, quick and easy. All you need is a Verified Alberta.ca Account. If you don’t have an Alberta.ca Account, create one here.
