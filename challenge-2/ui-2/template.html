Classify on arrival

Category, subcategory, priority, sentiment. Tool-enforced output via Vertex Claude. Confidence scored so the human reviewer can sort doubt to the top.

Resolve or draft

Auto-resolve writes a complete reply and sends. Draft-reply queues a polished response for the human agent. Both keep tone and language matched to the customer.

Escalate cleanly

Complex tickets get a three-sentence summary, the customer's plan tier, and the exact policy the human needs to look up. No raw transcripts dumped onto the queue.
