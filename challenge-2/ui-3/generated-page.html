Three paths. Always one.

auto_resolve

Reply and close

Simple billing question, known feature ask, password reset. The agent writes a complete answer in the customer's language and resolves the ticket.

draft_reply

Hand off the draft

Account changes, mid-complexity technical issues. The agent prepares a polished response; the human reviewer sends with one click or edits in place.

escalate

Brief the human

Outages, complaints, ambiguous policy. Three-sentence summary, plan tier, the exact policy reference. No transcript dumps.
