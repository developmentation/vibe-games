Sixty-five eligibility fields One profile

The eligibility schema covers identity, residency, household, financial, medical, expenses, banking, and consent. Each leaf declares which document types can supply its value. The agent extracts what is on the page, merges across passes, and resolves collisions to the more detailed value.

Step 01

Age and residency

Date of birth from the Alberta ID. Address from the utility bill. Both checks pass before the agent looks at anything else.

Step 02

Medical eligibility

AISH Medical Report Part B, plus the specialist consult. Diagnosis is permanent, work capacity is impaired. Confidence and citation per field.

Step 03

Income and assets

Monthly income from the pay stub or NOA. Liquid assets from chequing and savings. Compared against the AISH threshold automatically.
